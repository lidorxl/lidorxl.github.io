Privacy Policy

XLGG Tech games and apps · Last updated: November 4, 2026

This policy explains what information XLGG Tech ("we", "us") collects when you play our games and apps (the "Games"), including our virtual and mixed reality games on Meta Quest and Steam and their companion phone apps, and how that information is used. We keep data collection to what the Games need to run, to let you play with friends, and to fix crashes. We do not sell personal information and we do not show ads.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhyWho processes it
Online co-op connection data: your IP address and a random, anonymous player ID created for the session. To connect you to friends' games over the internet (relay servers) and keep the session running. Unity Technologies (Unity Authentication and Relay)
Room layout for co-op: in mixed reality, the shape of your play space (walls, floor, and the size and position of furniture from your headset's room setup). No camera images or video. Sent directly to the friends in your co-op session so their game can show your room and line up with it. It is not stored by us. The other players' devices; shared spatial anchors are handled by Meta when you play in the same room
Crash and error reports: device model, operating system, game version, the error or crash details, and the last lines of the game's log. To find and fix crashes and bugs. Unity Technologies (Unity Cloud Diagnostics)
Platform account features: your platform user ID, and, if you use them, achievements, friend invites and "join a friend" presence. To check that you own the game, unlock achievements, and let friends invite and join you. Meta Platforms (Meta Horizon Store) or Valve (Steam)
Phone companion app camera: the camera view and depth information. Used only on your phone to show the game in your room and line it up with the headset's room. Camera images are never sent or stored by us. Processed on your phone (Google ARCore / Apple ARKit)
Game progress and settings: scores, unlocks, upgrades, settings, and performance logs. Saved only on your device so you can continue where you left off. Your device only

2. What we don't collect

  • We don't collect your name, e-mail, or contact details through the Games.
  • We don't record or upload camera images, passthrough video, or audio.
  • We don't use advertising IDs, ad networks, or cross-app tracking.

3. Third-party services

The services above handle data under their own privacy policies: Unity, Meta, Valve (Steam), Google (ARCore) and Apple (ARKit).

4. How long we keep data

Co-op connection data and room layouts last only for the play session. Crash reports are kept by Unity Cloud Diagnostics only as long as needed to fix the problems they show, under Unity's retention rules. Data saved on your device stays there until you delete the Game.

5. Children

The Games are made for teens and adults and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the Games' online features, contact us and we will delete any related data.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can play without online co-op; then no connection data is sent.
  • You can delete all locally saved data by uninstalling the Game.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete data linked to you, or ask any question about this policy, by e-mailing xlggtech@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. Include your platform user name so we can find related crash reports.
  • If you live in the EU, UK or another region with data-protection laws, you have the rights those laws give you, including the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

7. Security

Online co-op traffic is encrypted (DTLS) through Unity Relay. Crash reports are sent over HTTPS.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell players in the Game or on its store page.
